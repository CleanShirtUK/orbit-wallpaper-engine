# Third-Party Notices

Orbit Wallpaper Engine contains or interoperates with third-party projects. This file records the principal third-party material identified during release preparation.

## KDE Shader Wallpaper

Project:

https://github.com/y4my4my4m/kde-shader-wallpaper

KDE Shader Wallpaper is the upstream source used by Orbit's optional online shader catalogue and is a major inspiration for the shader-library functionality.

The upstream project states that it is licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later**.

Orbit does not bundle the catalogue's third-party shaders. Individual shaders are downloaded on demand and can carry licence/attribution terms independent of the KDE Shader Wallpaper application itself.

See `SHADER_POLICY.md`.

Orbit Wallpaper Engine is independent and is not affiliated with KDE or the KDE Shader Wallpaper project.

## Quickshell

Project:

https://quickshell.org/
https://git.outfoxxed.me/quickshell/quickshell

Quickshell is used at runtime for Orbit's optional QML graphical settings application.

The Quickshell project states that it is licensed under **GNU LGPL 3** (with GPL-licensed portions identified by upstream).

Orbit does not vendor the Quickshell source code.
